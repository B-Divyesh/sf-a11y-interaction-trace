Policy / effective 27 August 2026

Privacy stays on your machine.

A11y Interaction Trace is local-first and has no server-side session storage.

Short version: we do not receive your traces. The extension does not send analytics, screenshots, page contents, or interaction data anywhere.

What the extension records

Only after you explicitly start a session, the extension records sanitized keyboard actions, focus targets, selected DOM-derived accessibility properties, the tested page URL and title, viewport metadata, and—if you opt in—screenshots of the visible tested tab.

Masking

Printable keys are recorded as “Character,” not their value. Password, payment, one-time-code, and developer-marked private fields are labeled sensitive and never include their values. Accessible names can still contain page-provided text, so review a trace before sharing it.

Storage and deletion

The active and latest session are stored in your browser’s local extension storage. Export creates a standalone HTML file at a location you choose. “Clear local trace” removes extension storage; exported files remain under your control.

Network and third parties

The extension has no telemetry, account system, cloud API, advertising, or third-party runtime. The public website also uses no analytics or tracking. Browser stores or source hosts may apply their own policies when you download the extension.

Permissions

activeTab and site access let the extension observe the tab you choose to test. storage keeps the current trace locally. downloads saves the exported viewer. Screenshot capture is off unless you enable it for that session.

Contact and changes

Questions and policy changes are tracked in the project’s public source repository. Material changes will update the effective date above.
