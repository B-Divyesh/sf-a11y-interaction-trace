Built for handoff

Evidence, not surveillance.

The recorder only runs after you start it. A toolbar badge and page dock stay visible. Stop from either place.

01

Words stay private

Printable keys become “Character.” Password and payment fields are marked sensitive; values never enter the trace.

02

Pixels are opt-in

Screenshots start off. When enabled, the extension captures only the visible tested tab—not your desktop or other tabs.

03

The file explains itself

The exported viewer includes timing, focus, role/name/state context, and honest scope notes. It needs no hosted app.

“Small enough to attach. Specific enough to act on.”
  • Everything stays in browser-local storage until you clear it.
  • No analytics, trackers, account, or third-party runtime.
  • Works offline after installation; exports open offline anywhere.
  • Narrow snapshots are labeled—not passed off as a full OS accessibility tree.

Install unpacked / v1

From zip to first trace.

Download and unzip. In Chrome or Edge, open the Extensions page, enable Developer mode, choose “Load unpacked,” and select the extracted folder. Pin the moss-path icon, open your test page, and press Start.
